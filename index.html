          Good to know

          • It is taxed as income: the withdrawal is added to your pay and taxed at your marginal rate.
          • SARS can reduce it to settle any tax debt you owe.
          • It shrinks the tax-free lump sum you can take at retirement.
          • Keep a separate emergency fund so you never have to raid your retirement.

          Things to avoid

          • Funds with high fees: a 1% yearly fee can cost about a quarter of your final balance over 30 years.
          • Lifestyle creep: when income rises, raise your savings rate first.
          • Early withdrawals: keep an emergency fund so you never sell investments at a bad time.
          • Chasing the market: steady, automatic contributions beat timing it.
